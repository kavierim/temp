What is Motion Magnification?

Motion Magnification is a powerful computer vision technique that allows us to see tiny, often invisible movements in video. By analyzing sub-pixel changes over time, we can amplify specific frequencies of movement, revealing things like a person's pulse, the subtle sway of a building, or the vibrations of an engine.

How This Tool Works

This application implements a real-time version of Eulerian Video Magnification. Unlike traditional "feature tracking" which follows specific objects, Eulerian methods look at pixels as a fixed grid. By comparing current frames with a temporal average (smoothing), we isolate the "motion signal" and multiply it (gain) before rendering it back onto the screen.

Scientific Use Cases

  • Biometrics: Detecting heart rates through skin color changes and micro-movements.
  • Structural Engineering: Identifying resonant frequencies in bridges or cranes.
  • Mechanical Maintenance: Troubleshooting imbalanced rotors or vibrating machinery.

Key Features

  • Frequency Filtering: Isolate specific vibration speeds using the Smoothing and Noise Filter controls.
  • Motion Burning: Visualize paths of movement over time with custom persistence.
  • Digital Zoom: Focus amplification on specific areas of interest.
  • Multi-Device Support: Switch between internal and external USB webcams.
